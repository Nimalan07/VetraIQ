## General Service Ball Valve (GB Series)

### Manufacturer: Swagelok
### Category: Ball Valve

---

## 1. Product Overview
The **Swagelok GB Series General Service Ball Valve** is engineered for reliable, high-integrity service in a wide range of industrial applications. Designed for working pressures up to **6,000 psig (413 bar)**, these valves feature a mechanically locked end-screw design for enhanced safety and dual-direction flow capability. With options for fire-safe compliance (API 607) and compatibility with sour gas environments (NACE MR0175/ISO 15156), the GB series represents a highly durable and customizable flow control solution.
