## 1. Product Overview
The **Siemens SIMOTICS GP** (General Purpose) is a premium, high-efficiency low-voltage motor with a lightweight aluminum housing. Operating with an IE3 Premium Efficiency rating, it delivers 11 kW (15 HP) of mechanical power. Designed for continuous industrial operations under the IM B3 mounting standard, this self-ventilated motor features IP55 dust and water protection, making it ideal for pumps, fans, and compressors.
