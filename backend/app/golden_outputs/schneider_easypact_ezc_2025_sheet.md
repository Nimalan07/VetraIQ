## EasyPact EZC Molded Case Circuit Breaker

### Manufacturer: Schneider Electric
### Category: Circuit Breaker

---

## 1. Product Overview
The **Schneider Electric EasyPact EZC** series is a compact, robust Molded Case Circuit Breaker (MCCB) designed for electrical distribution protection. Featuring a 3-pole design with a 60A rated current and a breaking capacity of 30kA, it provides thermal-magnetic protection against overloads and short circuits. Its standardized footprint and certifications make it ideal for commercial, industrial, and marine panelboard applications.
